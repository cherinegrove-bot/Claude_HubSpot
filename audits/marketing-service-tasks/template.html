Findings

C1

PPC and review workflows share one suppression list, so each company gets only one of its two tasks

Critical
What
All four task workflows use list 5466 as their suppression list and as a "remove from workflow" first step, and all four add the company to 5466 after creating the task. All {{N.ppc}} PPC-eligible companies are also review candidates.
Impact
  • If the manual review workflows run before 10:00 on 5 Dec, every PPC company is pulled out of the PPC workflow and Mohammad gets no tasks.
  • If the PPC workflow fires first, those companies are suppressed and cannot be enrolled in the review workflows, so the MPM gets no review task.
  • The sheet says PPC runs monthly. The 45-day suppression would also block the January PPC task.
Sheet says
PPC is "Mohammad's own task… runs alongside the master review, on the same facility ticket & cycle", monthly.
Suggested fix
Give the review workflows their own suppression list. Take list 5466 out of the PPC workflow (suppression, first branch and the add-to-list step), or give PPC a separate list with a shorter release if it needs one.
C2

{{N.supp}} companies are already in the suppression list and will be skipped on 5 December

Critical
What
List 5466 already has {{N.supp}} members, all added on 23 Nov between 19:17 and 19:31 UTC. All of them qualify today (At Risk or Improving). Only {{N.supp_nov5m}} received a review task on 5 Nov; the other {{N.supp_not_nov5m}} received only a PPC task, or nothing.
Impact
  • None of the {{N.supp}} gets a review or PPC task on 5 Dec.
  • If the suppression workflow enrolled them on 23 Nov, they leave the list about 7 Jan, after the 5 Jan run.
  • If the suppression workflow was switched on after 23 Nov, they were never enrolled (it triggers on joining the list) and will stay suppressed until removed by hand.
Check
Decide whether these companies should be skipped this cycle. Check the suppression workflow's enrollment history for them. Empty the list before 5 Dec if they are meant to get tasks.
C3

Berat's subtasks were not assigned to Berat

Critical
What
On the 5 Nov review tasks, all subtasks had no owner, including Google Business Profile and SpareFoot (if active), which the sheet assigns to Berat (owner ID 78394500).
Rule
A subtask with a different owner from the master task is assigned to that person. A subtask with the same owner as the master is left unassigned.
Expected
  • Google Business Profile: Berat
  • SpareFoot (if active): Berat (SpareFoot = Yes branch only)
  • Website Health & Optimization, Website Performance, HubSpot Marketing Card: unassigned
This applies to all 8 owner branches in each of the 3 review workflows (24 task actions).
Sheet gap
Website Health & Optimization is "MPM (+ Berat on content items)". A subtask can only have one owner. Either split Berat's three content checks into their own subtask, or leave the whole subtask with the MPM and say so in the sheet.
C4

Review workflows have no enrollment rules, so the right companies depend on who is picked by hand

Critical
What
At Risk, Improving and Standalone are manual-only. Nothing in them checks Trend to Goal, Customer Tier, Company Status or the ticket lane. Any company enrolled gets a task.
Risk
{{C.imprinel}} live companies are Improving but are Tier 2.2, Tier 3 or N/A and must not go into the Improving workflow. Offboarding, Lost or On Hold companies would also get tasks if selected.
Suggested fix
Enroll from saved lists that match the sheet exactly, or add a guard branch at the start of each workflow that exits companies outside its rule. The three review workflows are switched off and must be switched on before manual enrollment works.

H6

PPC workflow enrolls now from today's data and needs hand edits every month

High
What
  • It is on and list-based, so eligible companies are enrolled today and wait for a fixed date (5 Dec 10:00). "Unenroll if criteria no longer met" is off: a company whose Trend to Goal changes before 5 Dec still gets the task.
  • A company that becomes eligible after 10:00 on 5 Dec exits with no task (the delay branch only continues on "date met as planned").
  • No re-enrollment triggers are set, so the same companies will not re-enroll for January, and the fixed date must be edited monthly.
  • The PPC task has no queue and an empty description. Its checklist lives only in subtasks.
Checklist drift
On 5 Nov the PPC subtasks still included "PPC budget in line with suggested budget — recommend if not". The sheet moved that item to the HubSpot Marketing Card subtask. Confirm the current PPC subtasks.
