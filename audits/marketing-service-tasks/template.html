Fix

M1

"Next Performance Review due" adds 1 month, but reviews run every 2 months

Medium
What
The new ticket property next_performance_review_due is a calculation: add_time(last_review_date, 1, 'month').
Example
A December review completed on 20 Jan shows the next review as due 20 Feb. The next review task is not created until 5 Apr.
Check
If the date should match the 2-month cycle, change the 1 to 2. Also, the older empty date property "Next review due" (next_review_due) still exists on tickets. Hide or archive it so nobody fills in the wrong one.

Medium
What
